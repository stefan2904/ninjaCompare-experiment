## Overview
Users can add products to a basket. Products are automatically grouped by store, creating one basket per store. The feature compares the total price of the different store baskets side by side.

## Implementation Summary

### Files Modified
- `dashboard/index.html` – Added basket FAB, comparison modal, Chart.js CDN, 6th table column
- `dashboard/app.js` – Full rewrite with basket management, comparison view, price history charts, hash-based sharing
- `dashboard/styles.css` – Added comprehensive styles for basket buttons, FAB, comparison modal, charts, toast notifications, responsive breakpoints

### Features Implemented
1. **Add to Basket** – "+" button on each product row in the table; toggles to "✓" when in basket; rows highlighted green
2. **Floating Compare Button** – FAB in bottom-right appears when basket is non-empty, shows item count
3. **Comparison Modal** – Full-screen overlay showing:
   - Summary bar with product count and savings highlight
   - Store baskets sorted by total price (cheapest first)
   - Per-product pricing with remove buttons
   - Cheapest store badge and savings vs most expensive
4. **Price History Charts** (Chart.js):
   - Main chart: total basket price per store over time
   - Individual product sparkline charts for items with 2+ history entries
   - Graceful fallback message when insufficient history data
5. **localStorage Persistence** – Basket IDs saved to `ninjaCompare_basket` key
6. **Hash-based Sharing** – `#basket=id1,id2,...` URL fragment; Share button copies link; auto-opens comparison on hash navigation
7. **Keyboard/UX** – Escape closes modal, backdrop click closes, confirm before clear
